## Summary
This website is owned and managed by NITK Surathkal Alumni Association and is maintained by a voluntary group of alumni on behalf of the Association. We collect information only if you choose to share it. You retain rights to your personal data and may request deletion. Cookies are used for basic functionality and analytics. ## Detailed Policy

### Ownership and Scope
This website is owned and managed by NITK Surathkal Alumni Association ("Operator") and maintained by a voluntary group of alumni. This policy governs how personal data is collected, used, and protected.

### Information We Collect
We collect only information voluntarily provided by users, including but not limited to name, email, and profile details.

We may also collect:
- Device and browser metadata
- Usage logs and interaction data
- Error and diagnostic data

### Use of Information
We use information to:
- Provide and improve services
- Enable member networking
- Communicate relevant updates
- Maintain system security

We do not sell or rent personal data.

### Data Sharing
Information may be shared only:
- With service providers supporting operations (if any)
- When required by law
- To protect rights, safety, or prevent fraud

### Data Security
We implement reasonable safeguards against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss of data. However, no system is completely secure.

### User Rights
Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal data by contacting us.

### Cookies
Cookies are used for session management and analytics. Users may disable cookies via browser settings.

### Changes to Policy
We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance.
