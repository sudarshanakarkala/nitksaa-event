## Summary
Refunds and cancellations depend on each event. The terms shown on an event's page at the time you register apply to that registration. Where an event allows it, you can cancel from My Events and any eligible refund goes back to your original payment method.

## Policy

### Event-specific terms
Each event sets its own cancellation and refund terms, which may include a cancellation deadline, a non-refundable portion, or no refunds at all. These terms are shown on the event page before you pay. Where an event's terms differ from this page, the event's terms apply.

### Cancelling a registration
Where an event allows cancellation, you can cancel from My Events before the event's cancellation deadline. After the deadline, or once the event has started, registrations cannot be cancelled through the app.

### Refunds
Eligible refunds are made to the original payment method through our payment provider. Once a refund is issued, it usually reaches your account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank or card issuer. Payment gateway or convenience charges may not be refundable.

### Events cancelled or changed by the organisers
If an event is cancelled by the organisers, registered attendees will receive a full refund of the amount paid. If an event is significantly changed (for example, its date or venue), we will let registered attendees know and explain their options.

### No-shows and transfers
Not attending an event does not by itself qualify for a refund. Registrations may not be transferred to another person unless the event's terms allow it.

### Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. The version in effect when you register applies to that registration.

### Contact Information
For questions about a refund or cancellation, email nitksaa.infra@gmail.com with your name, the event, and your registration details.
