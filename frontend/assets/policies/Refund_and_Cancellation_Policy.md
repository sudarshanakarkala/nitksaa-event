## Summary
Whether a registration can be cancelled and refunded depends on the event.

## Policy

### Who can get a refund
Refunds are made only for verified registrations: registered through this app, with a payment successfully received through our payment gateway.

### How much
Any refund is the amount paid, less payment processing charges and event management fees where these apply. Some events may not offer refunds, or may offer them only until a cut-off.

### How to cancel
Where the event allows it, cancel from My Events.

### When you'll receive it
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Timing depends on clearance by the payment gateway and your bank, usually 5–7 working days after the refund is processed.

### No-shows and transfers
Not attending does not by itself qualify for a refund. Registrations cannot be transferred to another person unless the event allows it.

### Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated from time to time.

### Questions
Use the Feedback page in this app.
