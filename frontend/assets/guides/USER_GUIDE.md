# NITKSAA Events — How to register

**For:** anyone attending a NITKSAA event, alumni or not. **Site:** events.nitkalumni.in. **Updated:** 19 Oct 2026.
Words in **bold** are what you see on screen.

---

## 1. Find the event

- Open **events.nitkalumni.in**. You can browse and open any event without signing in.
- The **Events** page shows **Upcoming** events first. **Open** shows the ones taking registrations.
- Tap **View Details** to open an event: date, time, place, price and what it's about.
- **Dates:** tap **This week**, **This month** or **Next 90 days**; for other dates, **Pick dates** opens a slider and From – To boxes.
- Got a link like **events.nitkalumni.in/e/…** (on WhatsApp, or from another site)? It opens that event directly. If it says "This event isn't available", the link may be mistyped or the event isn't open yet.
- **Tell a friend:** on the event page, **Share** (on a phone: WhatsApp and others) or **Copy link** (on a computer).

**Big events (e.g. NITKonnect):**
- **Agenda:** grouped by time. When several sessions run at once ("6 sessions at once"), they sit side by side on a computer; on a phone, swipe sideways ("1 of 6"). The hall shows when known; tea and lunch breaks show as bands. A session may show its type and track (e.g. "Panel · Career Konnect") and who presents it; when there are several tracks, tap one at the top of the agenda to see only its sessions, or **All**.
- **Who's speaking:** each session lists its speakers under it (photo or initials, name, title and company; the moderator is marked). Panels show the first 3; tap **+N more** to see the rest.
- **Speakers:** the first 12, then **Show all**; type a name or company in **Search speakers**.
- **Sponsors:** grouped by tier (title sponsor first); some show their role, e.g. "Co-presenter".

## 2. Sign in

You need to sign in to register. On the event page, tap **Sign in to register**; after signing in you come back to the same event.

| Your email | How to sign in |
|---|---|
| Gmail or any Google account | **Continue with Google** |
| Any other email (Outlook, work, etc.) | Type it under **Sign in with email** › **Send sign-in link**. Open the email and tap the link |

**Sign-in link tips:**
- Open the link on the **same phone or computer** where you asked for it.
- On a phone, open it in **Chrome** or **Safari** (long-press the link › Open in Chrome, or ⋮ › Open in browser), not inside the email app.
- No email? Check spam. The link works once; ask for a new one with **Resend link**.

---

## 3. Register

1. On the event page, tap **Register**.
2. Check **Your details**:
   - **Name:** alumni see the name from their alumni record; others type their full name.
   - **Email:** your sign-in email.
   - **Phone:** required, so the organisers can reach you.
   - **Note** (optional): anything the organisers should know.
3. **Bringing guests?** Tap **Add a guest** and type each guest's full name (if the event allows guests; up to 4). Each guest gets their own QR.
4. Check the **Total** (e.g. "3 people × ₹500 = ₹1,500").
5. **Free event:** tap **Register**. Done.
   **Paid event:** tap **Continue to payment** › **Proceed to Payment** › pay in the Razorpay window (UPI, card or net banking).

You won't get an email. Your registration and QR are in the app (§4).

**If payment doesn't finish:**
- Closed the window or the payment failed? Your seat is held for a short time. On the event page, tap **Complete payment**.
- "Payment received, confirming…": wait a minute and reload the page. **Don't pay again.**

---

## 4. Your QR badge (entry pass)

Every person, guests included, has their own QR. Show it at the entrance.

- **My Events** (tap your initials at the top right › **My Events**) › the event › **QR badge**: shows your QR and every guest's.
- Or on the event page: **View QR badge** (yours), and **QR** next to each guest.
- Tip: take a screenshot before you leave, in case the venue has poor signal.
- Guests can be let in from your phone, or send each guest a screenshot of their QR.

## 5. Change of plans

| You want to | Do this | Refund (paid events) |
|---|---|---|
| Drop one guest | Event page › **✕** next to the guest › **Cancel guest** | That guest's share, automatically |
| Cancel your registration | Event card (Events or My Events) › **Unregister** › **Unregister**. Cancels your guests too | Everything not already refunded, automatically |
| Add a guest later | Unregister, then register again with the guest | — |

- You can cancel **until the event starts**, even after registration has closed.
- Refunds follow the [Refund Policy](https://events.nitkalumni.in/refund) and go back to the way you paid; the bank decides how long it takes.
- See where your refund is: **My Events** › **View refund status**.

| Refund status | Means |
|---|---|
| Refund on its way | Started; with your bank soon |
| Refund delayed, retrying | Held up for now; the app keeps trying |
| Refunded | Done. Use the **Refund reference** with your bank if needed |
| Refund needs attention | It didn't go through. Send us a note through **Feedback** |

---

## 6. Online events

Once registered, tap **Join meeting** on the event page or in My Events. The link is only shown to registered people.

---

## 7. NITiKA (ask a question)

When signed in, tap the chat icon at the top to ask NITiKA, e.g. "What's coming up?", "What have I registered for?" or "How do refunds work?". Chats aren't saved.

NITiKA answers questions only. It can't register you, cancel a registration, take a payment or change your details: use the pages above for that.

---

## 8. Questions and problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No **Register** button | The page says why: "Registration is not open yet", "Registration is closed" or "Event is at full capacity" |
| Asked for your email again after tapping the sign-in link | You opened it inside the email app. Open it in Chrome or Safari (§2) |
| "This is a Google (Gmail) address" | Use **Continue with Google** |
| Paid, but not shown as registered | Wait a minute and reload. Still not? Send **Feedback** (don't pay again) |
| "This event has started, so you can't unregister now." | Cancelling closes when the event starts |
| Can't find your QR | **My Events** › **QR badge** |
| Want to organise an event | Events are set up by organisers. Ask for organiser access through **Feedback** |
| Anything else | **Feedback** at the bottom of every page (you can attach screenshots). On a phone the bottom links slide away while you scroll down a list; scroll up a little, or to the end, to bring them back |
